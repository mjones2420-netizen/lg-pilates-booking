6. Frequently Asked Questions

URL: lg-pilates.co.uk/frequently-asked-questions/

An accordion FAQ widget with a search box. All 5 questions and answers, in order:

Do i need to bring a mat?

Yes if you have one. Don't worry if not, spare mats can be provided.

Is the class suitable for beginners?

The class is suitable for all levels. You will need to specify if you are a beginner to the instructor beforehand. The instructor can adapt exercises depending on your experience and ability.

What do i need to wear?

Wear comfortable clothing that you can move around in easily. In the colder months it is a good idea to wear layers that you can remove if necessary.

Do you offer a pay as you go option?

It is advised to book a block to secure your place. Pay as you go option is at a higher rate per class and is dependent on availability.
The blocks fill up fast, so to avoid disappointment, block booking is recommended.

What if i miss a class?

Where there is availability, you can make up your class in one of the other sessions. You have to make up your class within the block.
If there is no availability a catch up video class will be provided via a private YouTube link.
LG Pilates provides new catch up classes for every new block.

7. Terms and Conditions

URL: lg-pilates.co.uk/terms-and-conditions/ — linked from the footer of every page and from the How to Book page ("View T&Cs"), not from the main nav.

"Please read the following statement for terms and conditions"

  • Payment is to be made before the start of a block, no later than 48 hours.
  • Blocks will run between 4-6 weeks dependent on school holidays, instructor holiday or venue availability.
  • Advance notice will be given regarding block length and any changes to blocks or block prices.
  • Please make payment within the time frame stated on the invoice, to insure your space it is recommended payment is made promptly.
  • You pay for your block upfront regardless of how many sessions you attend.
  • If you cancel your block booking you must give the instructor at least 48 hours notice. Any cancellations after this time will be charged.
  • If you are aware you cannot make one of the sessions within the block, Please notify the instructor in advance and they will try to accommodate you into another class, although this will be dependent on availability. You have to make up your session within the block.
  • Classes can not be carried over into a new block.
  • Blocks can not be carried on to other blocks.
  • A recorded video will be available if you are unable to make up the session in person. There will be a new recording for each block.
  • Missed classes will not be refunded.
  • Classes can not be refunded due to weather snow/ heatwaves etc, but an online/ zoom class will be provided.
  • No participants under the age of 18
  • Participants are not to bring children to class.
  • You are responsible for any personal belongings that you bring to class.
  • Please arrive early for your class, the instructor has the right to refuse late participants.

"By booking your block you agree to accept the terms and conditions for LG Pilates."
